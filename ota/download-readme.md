# PureEQ

Personal sound for any Android phone (Android 9 or newer), in the spirit of Samsung's Adapt Sound
and realme's Golden Sound. A two-minute listening check and a short A/B taste test become one EQ per
ear, on top of your headphone model's lab-measured correction to the Harman target. Apply it to all
audio on the phone, or export it to Wavelet, Poweramp Equalizer, JamesDSP, ViPER4Android,
Equalizer APO and graphic EQs.

## Install

1. Open the download link on your phone and download the APK.
2. Open the downloaded file. When Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install
   unknown apps, then tap Install.
3. From then on PureEQ updates itself: new versions download, are checked (size, SHA-256, package
   and signing key) and install by themselves on Android 12 and newer once you allow it, or with
   one tap otherwise.

If Play Protect warns about an app it doesn't know, that's because PureEQ isn't on the Play Store;
you can choose to install anyway.

## Good to know

- Make one profile per pair of headphones, and turn off other sound effects first (Dolby Atmos,
  Adapt Sound, other EQ apps, your earbuds' own EQ).
- Your profiles and results stay on the phone. PureEQ goes online only to check for its own updates
  and, when you look up your headphone model, to download AutoEq's list of measured headphones and
  that model's measurement. Nothing about you or your hearing is sent.
- PureEQ personalises sound. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose hearing.
