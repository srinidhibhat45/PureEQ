# PureEQ privacy policy

Effective: <date of publication>

This policy covers PureEQ for Android (package `app.pureeq`), both the version from Google Play and the version from GitHub Releases.

**In short:** PureEQ works on your phone. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never sends your profiles, your test answers or anything else about you to the developer or to anyone else.

## What stays on your phone

- Your sound profiles, the answers and results of the listening check and the taste test, the headphone models you pick and your settings.
- A song you choose for the taste test is read on the phone and never uploaded.
- Exports are files that you save or share yourself, wherever you choose.

## Microphone

Before the listening check, PureEQ can measure the background noise in the room. If you allow the microphone, it listens for 3 seconds and works out the noise level on the phone. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent. Without the permission, PureEQ works the same and skips this check.

## What goes online

PureEQ connects to the internet only for these downloads, always over encrypted connections (HTTPS):

1. **Headphone data from AutoEq (both versions).** When you look up your headphone model, PureEQ downloads the public AutoEq index (about 850 KB, once) and the measurement or correction files for that model from jsDelivr (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`), or from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) when jsDelivr doesn't have them. The files are then kept on the phone.
2. **Update checks (GitHub version only).** The version installed from GitHub Releases checks `github.com` for a small update notice when you open it, every 30 minutes while the phone charges on Wi-Fi and once a day otherwise, and downloads new versions from GitHub. The Google Play version never does this; Google Play updates it.

These requests contain only the address of the file. Like any web request, they also show the server your IP address and a short app description ("PureEQ/version (Android version)"). jsDelivr and GitHub handle that under their own privacy policies; none of it reaches the developer.

## Backups

PureEQ allows Android's own backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy PureEQ's data (profiles, settings and downloaded headphone data) to your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. That copy is held by Google under Google's terms and the developer cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## What PureEQ does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, crash reporting or advertising services, and no tracking.
- No selling or sharing of data, since none is collected.
- No access to your contacts, location, photos or files, except an audio file or profile you pick yourself.

## Deleting your data

Delete profiles in the app at any time. Clearing PureEQ's storage or uninstalling it removes everything from the phone. A copy in Android's backup is managed from your Google account.

## Children

PureEQ is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
